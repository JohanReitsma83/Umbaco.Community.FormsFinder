This package uses Umbraco's internal index to quickly find all Umbraco Forms across your content. It shows a list of each form and the pages where they're used, making it easy to track forms on your site. 
Perfect for Umbraco developers and content editors, this tool helps you manage forms by giving you a clear view of where they're being used, so you can keep everything organized.
